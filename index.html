How to play

Pick three beasts. Once the battle starts they move and attack on their own; when one faints, the next one jumps in. Last team standing wins.

Speed sets how often a beast attacks. Physical moves use Attack against Defense, special moves use Sp. Atk against Sp. Def. Moves that match the user's element hit 1.5x harder.

P or Esc pauses. The seed box replays the exact same battle when you reuse a number.
